The Paper Trail

Raising Awareness About the Excessive Use of Paper And How We Can Consume Responsibly

About

My website aims to raise awareness about how students' everyday paper habits contribute to excessive paper consumption in schools. From unnecessary printing to wasting unused sheets, small actions can add up to a significant environmental problem. This website explores the impact of excessive paper use, including deforestation, resource depletion, and increased waste, while highlighting simple and effective solutions. Our goal is to encourage students, teachers, and schools to adopt more sustainable habits, reduce paper waste, and work towards a greener future. Because every sheet matters!
